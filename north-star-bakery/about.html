Our Story

North Star Bakery is a neighborhood bakery built around fresh, handmade baked goods and a welcoming community spirit. We take pride in creating breads, pastries, and cakes that bring people together for everyday moments and special celebrations.

Our Sourcing Philosophy

We believe great baking starts with thoughtful ingredients. North Star Bakery focuses on quality ingredients and a handmade approach to create baked goods our community can enjoy.

Staff Highlights

Our bakery team brings creativity, care, and a passion for baking to every shift. From preparing dough to decorating cakes, our staff works together to make each visit welcoming and each baked good special.
